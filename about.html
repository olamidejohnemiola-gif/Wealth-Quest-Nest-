Meet the Founder

Olamide John Emiola

The visionary and creative force behind THE WEALTH QUEST NEST, passionate about turning raw ideas into iconic, purpose-driven brands.

Rooted in faith, creativity, and innovation, Olamide built this platform to help businesses and individuals express excellence through design, branding, printing, web development, and digital experiences.

His journey began with a desire to create more than visuals. He wanted to help people birth purpose-filled ideas, build excellent brands, and break limits through creativity backed by faith.

In 2021, he made a deliberate choice to grow in skill and spirit, learning design, printing, coding, branding, and teaching. Every lesson became part of the blueprint for Wealth Quest Nest.

Here, we do not just design — we build legacies. From branding to web development, print to mentorship, everything is done with excellence, purpose, and integrity.
